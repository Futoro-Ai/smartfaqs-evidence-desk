# Annual leave schedule

Full-time annual leave allowance by completed years of service. One day is
eight working hours.

| Completed service | Annual hours | Equivalent days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-4 years | 80 hours | 10 days |
| 5-9 years | 104 hours | 13 days |
| 10+ years | 128 hours | 16 days |
